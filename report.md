EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-

This project analyzes revenue leakage and profitability performance of a multi-region e-commerce platform (2023–2025). The objective was to identify key financial risks, return behavior patterns, and category-level vulnerabilities affecting net profitability.

The analysis reveals that while overall revenue remains stable across three years, approximately 6% of revenue is lost annually due to product returns. Through a composite leakage risk scoring model, Fashion and Electronics categories were identified as the primary financial risk drivers.

Strategic interventions in these categories can significantly improve profitability and reduce revenue leakage.

KEY FINDINGS
-


1.Overall Return Rate & Financial Impact

The platform experiences a stable return rate of approximately 6.06%, resulting in an annual revenue leakage of nearly 4.4M. After adjusting for returns, net revenue reduces from 73.4M to 69.0M.

----------------------------------------
2.Fashion is the Primary Leakage Driver

Fashion exhibits a significantly higher return rate (12%) compared to other categories (5–6%).
It contributes the highest revenue loss (1.27M) and the highest profit loss (867K), making it the top financial risk category.

----------------------------------------
3.Electronics Shows Revenue Illusion

Although Electronics generates comparable revenue (10.4M), it operates at a low profit margin (11%).
This indicates margin vulnerability — moderate returns significantly reduce profitability.

----------------------------------------
4 Discounting Does Not Drive Returns

Return rates remain relatively constant across discount levels (0%–20%), indicating that over-discounting is not the primary cause of returns.

----------------------------------------
5️.Regional Impact is Financially Concentrated

While return rates are similar across regions, Europe contributes the highest revenue loss percentage, indicating higher financial exposure in that region.

----------------------------------------
6️.Profitability is Uneven Across Categories

Beauty maintains the highest profit margin (33%), making it the most financially stable category and a potential growth focus.

FINANCIAL IMPACT SUMMARY 
-

Total Gross Revenue (2023–2025): 73.41M

Revenue Lost Due to Returns: 4.41M

Net Revenue After Returns: 69.01M

Overall Profit: 15.44M

Overall Profit Margin: 22.38%

Although the overall margin remains healthy, category-level analysis reveals uneven profitability distribution. Fashion and Electronics categories significantly increase financial exposure due to high return rates and low margin buffers.

Reducing Fashion return rate from 12% to 8% alone could recover substantial profit without increasing sales volume.

STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
-

----------------------------------------
1️.Reduce Fashion Return Rate

Improve size accuracy and product descriptions

Introduce virtual fitting tools

Encourage store-credit returns instead of refunds

Analyze customer-level return behavior

--------------------------------------
2️ Improve Electronics Margin Protection

Negotiate supplier cost reduction

Reduce deep discount campaigns

Offer bundled pricing instead of price cuts

--------------------------------------
3️ Focus Growth on High-Margin Categories

Beauty and Home categories demonstrate strong profitability and low leakage risk. Marketing investment should prioritize these segments.

----------------------------------------
4 Monitor Seasonal Return Spikes

November and December show slight return rate increases. Implement stricter return policies during peak promotional seasons.


CONCLUSION
-

This analysis demonstrates that revenue growth alone does not guarantee profitability stability. While overall performance remains stable, structural return behavior in Fashion and margin vulnerability in Electronics represent significant financial risks.

By implementing targeted category-level strategies, the company can reduce leakage, improve profit resilience, and optimize long-term financial performance.
